How to play

New: drawing in the air. Draw any weapon or vehicle you like right on the screen, even while walking or running, without picking anything first. The game recognizes what you drew — plane, helicopter, car, truck, bus, motorcycle, tank, pistol, rifle, sword, knife, axe, hammer, bomb, boomerang — and its shape decides what it can do: a long barrel shoots far, two barrels make a shotgun, many barrels a minigun, a long blade reaches far. The recognizer learned from people’s drawings (Google’s Quick, Draw!, CC BY 4.0).

Photograph blueprint signs around the city, find cover, and draw them in the air — seventeen weapons and vehicles are waiting: from a paint pistol and a pencil rifle, through a ruler sword and a marker laser, to a sharpener minigun, a tank, a motorcycle and a helicopter. The better the drawing, the better the weapon! Walk into any shop, and on the boardwalk there’s a booth where you draw yourself a friend. You can take any car — just don’t let the police see.
