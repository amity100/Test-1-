## PROLOGUE · הארץ (0:00–0:40) — distant, mythic

1. **Black.** Wind; a single deep tone. Text: *לִפְנֵי כִּשְׁלֹשֶׁת אֲלָפִים שָׁנָה*
2. **Above the clouds.** The camera breaks through the cloud layer into dawn over the mountains of Judah: mist in
   the valleys, the Dead Sea glinting in the east (with the mountains of Moab beyond). Text:
   *שִׁבְטֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל יוֹשְׁבִים בְּאַרְצָם*
3. **Rachel's tomb.** An ancient road; Rachel's standing-stone pillar (מַצֵּבָה) alone in the first light; a
   shepherd and his flock pass in the distance.
   "וַתָּמָת רָחֵל וַתִּקָּבֵר בְּדֶרֶךְ אֶפְרָתָה הִוא בֵּית לָחֶם" (Gen 35:19)
4. **The threat.** West, on the coastal plain by the sea, a Philistine army marches in the dust; bronze glints;
   war drums rise. "וַתְּהִי הַמִּלְחָמָה חֲזָקָה עַל־פְּלִשְׁתִּים כֹּל יְמֵי שָׁאוּל" (1 Sam 14:52)
5. **The people.** The elders of Israel gathered at Ramah (Samuel's town, 1 Sam 7:17 — Ramathaim in the hill country of Ephraim, 1:1; its site is debated), before one old man (Samuel).
   "שִׂימָה־לָּנוּ מֶלֶךְ לְשָׁפְטֵנוּ כְּכָל־הַגּוֹיִם" (1 Sam 8:5)

## ACT I · המלך (0:40–1:40) — spectacle, then collapse

6. **A shofar blast — hard cut.** Out of a dust cloud an army returning from war: thousands on foot, spears,
   shields, drums. Caption: *הַגִּלְגָּל*
7. **Saul.** Low angle, slow motion, sun behind him: at the head of the army strides a man taller than all —
   bronze helmet under his arm, armour, the gold diadem, spear in hand. Caption: *שָׁאוּל בֶּן־קִישׁ · מֶלֶךְ יִשְׂרָאֵל*
   "מִשִּׁכְמוֹ וָמַעְלָה גָּבֹהַּ מִכָּל־הָעָם" (1 Sam 9:2)
8. **The peak.** Saul raises his spear to the sky; thousands roar and raise their spears. Music at its peak.
   "וְשָׁאוּל לָכַד הַמְּלוּכָה עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵל וַיִּלָּחֶם סָבִיב בְּכָל־אֹיְבָיו" (1 Sam 14:47)
9. **Silence.** The roar cuts out at once; only wind — and, faintly, the bleating and lowing of the spoil (15:14). At the head of the road an old man wrapped in a robe
   stands in the way; the army parts. Caption: *שְׁמוּאֵל*
10. **The tearing.** The giant king and the old prophet face to face. Samuel turns to go; Saul seizes the corner
    of his robe — it tears, in slow motion: threads snapping, dust in the light.
    "וַיִּסֹּב שְׁמוּאֵל לָלֶכֶת וַיַּחֲזֵק בִּכְנַף־מְעִילוֹ וַיִּקָּרַע" (1 Sam 15:27)
11. **The verdict.** Close on Samuel, hard and quiet.
    "קָרַע ה׳ אֶת־מַמְלְכוּת יִשְׂרָאֵל מֵעָלֶיךָ הַיּוֹם וּנְתָנָהּ לְרֵעֲךָ הַטּוֹב מִמֶּךָּ" (1 Sam 15:28)
12. **Saul.** A long close-up: the king at the height of his glory — shattered; the torn piece of cloth in his
    fist; the army behind him out of focus.

## TRANSITION · החיפוש (1:40–1:55)

13. The camera rises from Saul into the sky — above the army, the clouds, the whole land — and sweeps south over
    the hills like a gaze from above that is searching. The music turns from dark to warm.
    "בִּקֵּשׁ ה׳ לוֹ אִישׁ כִּלְבָבוֹ" (1 Sam 13:14)

## ACT II · הרועה (1:55–2:25)

14. **Bethlehem.** Down through the clouds into golden hour: Bethlehem on its ridge, the terraces, the flock on
    the slope.
15. **The figure.** On a rock above the flock, a lone figure seen from behind: staff planted, wind in his curls
    (the user's reference image). "עוֹד שָׁאַר הַקָּטָן וְהִנֵּה רֹעֶה בַּצֹּאן" (1 Sam 16:11)
16. **The face.** He turns his head — the first time we see his face, in warm light.
    "וְהוּא אַדְמוֹנִי עִם־יְפֵה עֵינַיִם וְטוֹב רֹאִי" (1 Sam 16:12)
17. **The contrast.** Wide: the small youth in the vast landscape — the answer to Saul, "taller than all the
    people". "כִּי הָאָדָם יִרְאֶה לַעֵינַיִם וַה׳ יִרְאֶה לַלֵּבָב" (1 Sam 16:7)

## HOOK · בסבך (2:25–2:40)

18. **Peace.** The flock grazes; a little lamb wanders to the edge of a dark thicket.
19. **The silence before.** The birds fall silent. Deep in the thicket something large shifts; heavy breathing;
    two eyes open in the dark. The lamb lifts its head.
20. **Smash cut to black + a deep hit:** DAVID · דָּוִד · *פֶּרֶק רִאשׁוֹן · הָרֹעֶה* (the film's name "הַטּוֹב מִמֶּךָּ" is itself a quotation: render it only from the catalog, id s1_15_28_better_than_you)
21. **The game begins:** open on David on his rock and the first objective.
