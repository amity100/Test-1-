# Director's notes on the rough cut (artifact version 4)

Written by the orchestrator after watching the whole rough cut frame by frame: 2 frames per second of film time,
desktop-high, 640×360, plus the cut teammate's 1 fps sheets at 640×360 (dev/screens/cut/contact_*.jpg). Filmstrips:
`scratchpad/watch/v4strips/*.jpg`. These notes back the user's verdict (see `docs/intro-script-v2.md`) and tell
each teammate exactly what is wrong. Everything below applies to the NEW cut wherever the same set, character or
system is reused.

## The five problems that run through the whole film

1. **It is static.** Most shots are a slow or locked camera on a scene where nothing moves: Rachel's stone
   (5 s, same frame), the coast wide (4.4 s, empty plain, the host invisible), the Philistine blocks (the ranks
   barely move), Ramah (8 s, the elders small and still), Gilgal dust wall (7 s), Saul "the king" (8 s: Saul
   centred and hardly moving), the verdict (9 s: identical frames), Saul alone (10 s: identical frames), David from
   behind (7.5 s), David's face (7 s), the lamb (12 s). A player reads this as a slideshow.
2. **It is far too long** (164.8 s) and slow: long black card (4.5 s), a 15 s rise over an empty pale desert into a
   white-out, a slow crane over hills where Bethlehem cannot be found, a pull-back over a repeating rock.
3. **The text looks like subtitles**: small, thin, low-contrast lines at the bottom and small cards in the corners;
   too many of them (14 text events); several only describe what we see (15:27, 14:47) or are weak (the tribes line,
   Gen 35:19 over a stone nobody has introduced).
4. **The characters and crowds read as game NPCs / mannequins** at every distance where they are seen: flat faces,
   stringy hair, the tunic weave like woven straw, Saul's scale coat like cardboard feathers, the Philistine host as
   bright rectangular blocks, the soldiers near the lens with doll faces.
5. **The acting does not fit the moments**: the key beats (the spear raised, the tear, the verdict, David's reveal)
   are small or slow gestures on still bodies; the army's roar is a uniform arm-raise; Samuel stands like a statue.

## Shot by shot

### 1 · Black time card (0–4.5 s)
Tiny thin line in the middle of a black screen for 4.5 s; nothing else. → In the new cut: 0.3 s of black, the time
card LARGE and luminous, carried over the first image.

### 2 · Judah flight (4.5–17.5 s)
The clouds are good; the camera's move is slow and the first 5 s after the black are dim and murky; the ridges in
the valley fog (from ≈10 s) are the best image of the prologue. The line *שִׁבְטֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל…* is small and weak.
→ Keep the look, make it one fast 7.5 s flight (race over the clouds, dive through, burst out over the ridges).

### 3 · Rachel's stone (17.5–25.7 s)
dawn: 1.6 s double-exposure dissolve, then a locked frame of the stone for 3 s — nothing moves. road: the camera
pushes through a blurred foreground olive branch (ghostly leaves across the frame), the stone is cut at the bottom
left; the shepherd placeholder is invisible. → 3 s, a low dolly through the grass to the stone, the flock and the
shepherd crossing behind it; a place card.

### 4 · The coast (25.7–33.2 s)
threat: 4.4 s of an empty plain — the host is not visible at all, nothing moves. glint: the host as three bright
rectangular blocks of identical figures on a flat plain, like toy soldiers; the camera does not move. → 2.5 s,
long lens, a lateral track along a COLUMN on a road (depth, not blocks), dust, bronze glints, heat haze.

### 5 · Ramah (33.2–40.8 s)
gate: a long 1 s dissolve that lays the Philistine blocks over the Ramah wall (a muddy double exposure), then a
wide wall with nothing happening. elders: a push toward the gate where two or three tiny figures stand still;
the elders are not readable as elders, Samuel is a speck; the verse is the only event. → 4 s: move in over the
elders' heads (they fill the lower third, faces and beards readable), one rises and demands with his arm up,
the others react, Samuel turns his face away (8:6).

### 6 · Gilgal dust wall (40.8–47.8 s)
A locked wide frame for 7 s: palms, a dust cloud, and the army as a thin band on the horizon; nothing arrives, no
shofar is seen, the "hard cut" lands on a still postcard. The place card is small in the top corner. → 3 s, low
and close to the front rank as it comes out of the dust at the lens, horns lifted and blown, the card large.

### 7 · The king (47.8–55.8 s)
Low angle with Saul centre-left among soldiers, but for 8 s the frame is the same: the slow motion (≈¼ speed) makes
him take about one step, the camera does not move, the soldiers around him are a static crowd. His name card
collides with the soldiers on the right; the verse is small at the bottom. → 4.5 s: the camera tracks backward
low in front of him while he strides (slow motion but the stride continuous), dust, the spear swinging, the cloak
and beard moving; the name card huge in the sky's negative space.
