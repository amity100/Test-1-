In short: (1) motion and animation must fit each situation and be alive, real and interesting — never static;
(2) **one minute at most**; (3) Saul, David, Samuel and the people need much more polish; (4) the titles must
be cinematic, fitting and strong; (5) nothing may look unfinished.

## Rules of this cut

1. **Length: 58 s including the title card** (film 53 s + title 5 s). No shot longer than 4.5 s. No static hold:
   every shot has a motivated camera move AND visible motion inside the frame (people, animals, cloth, hair, dust,
   light). If a frame 1 s later looks the same, the shot is wrong.
2. **Rhythm:** the prologue flows (dissolves, the music building); Act I hits hard (cuts on the beat, slow motion
   used twice only: Saul's stride and the tear); the verdict is quiet but still moves; David is calm but alive; the
   hook tightens for 4 s and smashes to the title.
3. **Show, don't caption:** no verse describes what the picture already shows (15:27 and 14:47 are dropped).
4. **Faces:** close-ups only where the story needs them (Saul G2/G7, Samuel G6, David D2), never longer than
   3.5 s, always with a sculpting light (the face rigs, rim light, shallow depth of field). Hero shots favour
   silhouettes against the light, low angles, dust and motion.
5. **Accuracy** exactly as before (`docs/visual-bible.md`): Israel on foot, bearded, no drums in Israel's
   ranks, Samuel's long uncut white hair, Saul ≈2 m with bronze scale coat, helmet under the arm, thin gold nezer,
   armlet, spear; Rachel's single standing stone; no Agag; nothing after 16:13.

## On-screen text — the new typography (cinematic, not subtitles)

Only these 11 text events. Verses exactly from the catalog (`verseArgs(id)`), narration only from
`src/content/introNarration.ts`; narration is never styled as a verse and never gets a reference.

* **Time card:** centred, large (≈4.5vw desktop), light serif (Frank Ruhl Libre 300), warm white; letters
  resolve out of a soft blur with the tracking closing from 0.6em to 0.28em over 2.5 s; a faint light bloom;
  it survives the fade into the clouds and melts away.
* **Place cards** (*קֶבֶר רָחֵל*, *הַגִּלְגָּל*): large serif (≈4vw), in the negative space of the frame, revealed
  by a soft mask wipe from the right (RTL) with a thin gold rule growing under it; 2.5 s, then dissolve.
* **Person cards** (*שָׁאוּל · מֶלֶךְ יִשְׂרָאֵל*, *שְׁמוּאֵל*): the name very large (≈6–7vw), gold-to-cream metal
  gradient, set against the empty side of the composition; the title under it in small letter-spaced type;
  a light sweep across the name once; 2.8 s.
* **Verses:** centred in the lower third but LARGE (≈2.8–3.2vw desktop, ≥20 px on phones), appearing
  **word by word** (80–140 ms stagger, synced to Samuel's lips in G6), a soft dark glow behind for legibility;
  the reference (e.g. *שְׁמוּאֵל א׳ ט״ו כ״ח*) small, letter-spaced, fading in after the words. 15:28 is split across
  the cut G6 → D1: the first half fades out with Samuel, the second half writes itself over David.
* **Title:** smash to black on the hit; *DAVID* forms from light (blur → sharp, a slow light sweep), *דָּוִד*
  beneath, a gold rule grows, *פֶּרֶק רִאשׁוֹן · הָרֹעֶה* last. 5 s, then the dissolve into gameplay.
* Phones: every card stays inside the safe areas and above the letterbox; portrait and landscape rules.
